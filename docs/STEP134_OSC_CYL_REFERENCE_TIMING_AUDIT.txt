Findings
--------
1. CAL42 is mechanically mapped through 16055375 terminal 10 to U11 pin 18
   OSC. The photographed reconstruction supports a 91-kOhm external-terminal
   relationship to unresolved CAL34. No evidence connects this node to the U9
   register timebase or proves any U11 internal oscillator behavior.

2. CAL56 is mechanically mapped through 16055376 terminal 13 to U12 pin 11
   CYL. The photographed functional network supports 7.5 kOhm to unresolved
   CAL53. Its voltage encoding remains unknown.

3. U12 REF pin 29 is electrically separate from CYL. Conditioned ESTHI/ESTLO
   reaches REF; U12 outputs INJREF and IGNREF to U9 and U11. This is the
   supported distributor-reference path.

4. Firmware reads reference occurrence from $3FFA, period from $3FC0, uses
   $3FC8 in spark-period processing, and derives RPM at $CDE6-$CE41. These are
   direct register semantics, but the U9 pin-to-register implementation is not
   documented.

5. PROM LC009 controls reference-period normalization. Separately, $002F & $18
   is compared with LC225 and a mismatch feeds Error 41. CAL56/U12 CYL and the
   latter check form a strong end-to-end consistency inference, not a proven
   direct encoding.

Step 134 establishes separate oscillator configuration, cylinder selection,

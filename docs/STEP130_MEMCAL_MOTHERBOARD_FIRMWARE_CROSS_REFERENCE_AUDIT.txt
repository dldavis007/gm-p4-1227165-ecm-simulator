BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 130 - MEMCAL/MOTHERBOARD/FIRMWARE CROSS-REFERENCE
======================================================================

Purpose
-------
Step 130 uses the Step-129 physical carrier mapping and functional resistor
networks to improve the ECM theory of operation without inventing U11/U12
internals or assigning meaning from resistance alone.

Main findings
-------------
1. CAL42 is mechanically mapped to 16055375 terminal 10 and directly traced to
   U11 pin 18 OSC. The 91-kOhm CAL42-CAL34 relationship is useful electrical
   evidence, but oscillator behavior remains unresolved.

2. CAL56 is mechanically mapped to 16055376 terminal 13 and directly traced to
   U12 pin 11 CYL. Firmware independently contains an eight-cylinder PROM code
   at LC009 and an MPU-status-versus-LC225 consistency check at $F682-$F68B,
   with Error 41 latching at $E6A1-$E6AA. The overall consistency relationship
   is strongly inferred; CAL56 voltage encoding and U12/MPU internals remain
   unresolved. CAL56 is not equated directly with LC009 or LC225.

3. CAL61 is mechanically mapped to 16055376 terminal 7 and directly reaches
   U11 pin 28 MAP, where the conditioned MAP sensor path also terminates. The
   scaling/threshold/selection consequence is unresolved.

4. CAL59 is mechanically mapped to 16055376 terminal 6 and has a direct
   100-Ohm-to-VIGN motherboard path with a shunt capacitor. Its reconstructed
   24-kOhm relationship to the CAL55/CAL57 common node provides a strong
   electrical validation anchor but does not prove a U11 function.

5. CAL29 and CAL32 are separate paths. CAL29 belongs to the analog ESC network
   that reaches U10 A/D channel AN9. CAL32 reaches U12 KNOCK, and U12's KNOCK
   output reaches U9. Listing-backed knock attack/recovery and Error 43 behavior
   are documented without inventing the custom-device conversion.

Evidence classification
-----------------------
The new path document explicitly distinguishes direct evidence, mechanically
derived mapping, strong inference, and unresolved behavior. It also retains the
existing F0-F4 hardware-fidelity vocabulary.

Source and baseline impact
--------------------------
Documentation/evidence only. No C source, tests, generated output, or behavioral
baseline was changed. The available evidence supports better cross-reference,
not a new U11/U12 simulation.

Remaining boundaries
--------------------
- undocumented U11 and U12 internal transfer functions;
- exact CAL56 electrical encoding and $002F bit provenance;
- precise relationship between analog ESC, digital KNOCK, U9 event counting,
  and all Error-43 state;
- CAL34/CAL53/J4 62-64 motherboard destinations;
- future 16055375 full terminal resistance matrix.

Conclusion
----------
Step 130 establishes the first explicit end-to-end MEMCAL path audit from
physical terminal mapping through motherboard nets to listing-backed firmware
behavior. It strengthens the theory of operation while keeping custom-device
behavior and resistor-only semantics properly unresolved.

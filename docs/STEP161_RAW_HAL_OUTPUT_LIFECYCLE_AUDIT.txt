Established output behavior
---------------------------
The executable power-on path clears the mapped MPU word window from $3FC0 up to
but not including $3FFA. Therefore the observed $3FCC, $3FD0, $3FD2, $3FD4,
$3FD6, and $3FD8 outputs are zero at the normal scheduler handoff. The startup
parallel-I/O sequence leaves $4004 at $98.

During natural key-off, the shutdown path clears the engine-running bit. The
next Segment-1 output passes take the listing's engine-off branch at
$EDBD-$EDF9. With field diagnostic mode clear, the firmware writes $D000 to all
five common PWM counters:

  $3FCC  AIR/ARC raw counter
  $3FD2  EGR raw counter
  $3FD4  acceleration-enrichment raw counter
  $3FD6  TCC raw counter
  $3FD8  canister-purge raw counter

That same branch clears only bit 1 in $4004. The remaining established control
bits therefore stay at their startup state, producing $98. The separate raw
injector command at $3FD0 remains zero, and IAC position bookkeeping completes
at the calibrated park value 144 established in Step 160.

After $D6EA latches software powerdown, an attempted IRQ must not modify any of
those observed outputs. A host reset acknowledgement then re-enters $C800 and
source-clears the MPU output window before returning to normal startup, while
$4004 is re-established at $98.

Regression contract
-------------------
The Step-161 regression contains nine checks covering:

1. initial MPU output-window clear, including the injector command;
2. initial $4004 startup value;
3. natural key-off arrival at software powerdown;
4. all five common PWM counters at engine-off $D000;
5. fan bit clear with $4004 control bits preserved;
6. zero injector command and IAC parked at 144;
7. complete observed-output stability across a blocked IRQ;
8. MPU output-window clear after retained restart;
9. restored $4004 startup value after retained restart.

Its FNV-1a signature covers shutdown duration, all five terminal PWM values,
$4004, injector command, IAC position, and the corresponding post-reset raw
output state:

  Step-161 raw-HAL output lifecycle signature: B22B98ED
  step-161 raw-HAL output lifecycle regression result: PASS (9/9)

The test saves and restores memory, statistics, lifecycle/vector/power/startup
traces, output-stage state, FMD and VOLT inputs, the software-powerdown latch,
and the IAC motor flag so later frozen scenarios cannot be contaminated.

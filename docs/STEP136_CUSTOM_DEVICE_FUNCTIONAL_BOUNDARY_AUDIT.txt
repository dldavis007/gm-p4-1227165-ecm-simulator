BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 136 - U11/U12 CUSTOM-DEVICE FUNCTIONAL-BOUNDARY AUDIT
===========================================================================

Purpose
-------
Step 136 consolidates the Step-130 through Step-135 MEMCAL, reference,
ignition/injection, MAP, OSC/CYL, and ESC/knock findings into an explicit
black-box functional envelope for U11 (16054995) and U12 (16034984). It does
not invent undocumented custom-device internals.

Main findings
-------------
1. U11 is now bounded as a MEMCAL-configured reference/sensor custom device.
   Direct visible anchors include INJREF pin 5, IGNREF pin 6, OSC pin 18, TPS
   pin 20, CTS pin 23, MAP pin 28, multiple CAL inputs, and INJ pin 19 to
   INJLIMP. Its internal injection/limiting rule, oscillator transfer, MAP
   transfer, thresholds and polarities remain unresolved.

2. U12 is bounded in two visible sections. The reference/control section
   receives REF, CYL, KNOCK, IGN, EST, BYPASS and INJLOOP and emits INJREF,
   IGNREF, ESTLOOP, KNOCK, IRQ and PUMP. The injector-driver section receives
   INJS, INJA, ~LIMP and current-sense signals and drives INJOUT/Q1. Pulse
   decoding, phase, polarity, current control and truth tables remain unknown.

3. CAL42 -> U11 OSC, CAL61/MAP -> U11 MAP, and CAL59/VIGN-related bias are
   direct/mechanically derived U11 configuration anchors. None is promoted to
   a processor-readable value or to the firmware load producer.

4. CAL56 -> U12 CYL remains separate from U12 REF. Firmware's $002F & $18
   versus LC225 consistency check and Error 41 provide a strongly supported
   end-to-end cylinder-mode relationship, but the CAL56 voltage encoding and
   $002F provenance remain unresolved. LC009 and LC225 are not equated with
   CAL56.

5. The distributor-reference envelope is direct at both ends: conditioned
   ESTHI/ESTLO reaches U12 REF; U12 emits INJREF/IGNREF to U9/U11; firmware
   consumes $3FFA/$3FC0/$3FC8 and derives RPM. The U12/U9 pulse and
   pin-to-register transformations remain bounded.

6. The EST/bypass and injection envelopes are now explicitly separated into
   processor-visible U9 register behavior versus unresolved U9/U12 electrical
   waveform generation and current limiting.

7. CAL32/U12 KNOCK -> U9 pin 42 and firmware counter $3FCA form a coherent
   strong inference, while the separate CAL29/U10 AN9 analog ESC path remains
   distinct. U12 threshold/filtering and U9 pin/register mapping are not
   invented.

Theory-of-operation consequence
-------------------------------
The accumulated evidence is sufficient to describe U11 and U12 as black-box
subsystems with known external roles and known firmware-facing consequences.
It is not sufficient to emulate their comparators, counters, waveform phase,
thresholds, polarity, oscillator frequency, or current-control algorithms.
This is an important closure point: unknown internals no longer prevent a
coherent whole-ECM theory, provided the unknown transformations stay explicit.

Source and baseline impact
--------------------------
Documentation/evidence only. No C source, tests, generated output, or frozen
behavioral baseline is changed. The current F4 custom-device boundary remains
the correct simulation treatment.

Highest-value remaining evidence
--------------------------------
- Trace or measure CAL34 and CAL53 references.
- Measure CAL42/U11 OSC and CAL56/U12 CYL on a running ECM.
- Observe U12 REF, INJREF and IGNREF simultaneously.
- Establish U9 pin-to-register/status mapping by independent documentation or
  controlled hardware testing.
- Determine meanings/polarities of INJS, INJA, INJLIMP, INJLOOP and ~LIMP.
- Characterize U11's use of OSC/MAP/TPS/CTS/reference/CAL inputs only if direct
  evidence becomes available.

Conclusion
----------
Step 136 closes the descriptive U11/U12 boundary at the highest fidelity the
current evidence supports: external pins and networks are direct, several
end-to-end system relationships are strongly inferred, and undocumented
internal transformations remain explicit F4 boundaries rather than guessed
behavior.

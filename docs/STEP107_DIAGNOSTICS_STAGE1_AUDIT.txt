Deliberate integration boundary
-------------------------------
The listing executes LE4F7 diagnostic qualification immediately before the
logger.  That routine qualifies ERR13, ERR21, ERR24, ERR32, ERR33/34 and later
faults.  The listing can also branch from the logger to LF10E, a distinct
flash-code state machine.  Neither is silently skipped in scheduler execution.

For that reason `segD_diagnostics()` remains an explicitly documented empty
scheduler placeholder in Step 107.  The new units are scheduler-ready, but
they are exercised only by focused regression until LE4F7 and LF10E are
translated and can be connected in literal order.

Objective
---------

Findings
--------
1. Conditioned distributor activity reaches U12 pin 29 REF. U12's visible INJREF/IGNREF outputs reach U9 and U11. The exact U12 pulse transformation remains unresolved.

2. Firmware directly consumes U9-window reference status and timing: `$3FFA` reference occurrence, `$3FC0` reference-period state, `$3FC8` spark/reference-period state, and related `$3FEC` timing state.

3. `$CDE6-$CE41` normalizes reference period and derives RPM. `LC009=$00` selects the supplied eight-cylinder software normalization path. This PROM byte is not equated with the physical CAL56 voltage.

4. CAL56 reaches U12 CYL and the firmware separately compares `$002F & $18` with `LC225=$00`. The end-to-end cylinder/fuel-mode consistency role is strong inference; the electrical/digital encoding remains unresolved.

5. CAL42/U11 OSC remains separate from U9 reference counters and ordinary IRQ timing. No unsupported timebase relationship was introduced.

6. The even 12.5-ms scheduler path consumes reference/RPM/dwell state, but distributor reference edges are engine events rather than scheduler events.

7. Spark production combines listing-backed calibrated terms and mode corrections. `$D20C-$D267` converts the desired spark relationship into U9 timing quantities.

8. The established spark/dwell register group is `$3FDC`, `$3FE4`, `$3FE6`, `$3FE8`, `$3FEC`, `$3FF6`, with `$3FFC` used for EST/bypass/control state. These are processor-visible semantics, not an internal U9 timer specification.

9. U9 IGN and U12 IGN/EST/BYPASS/ESTLOOP form a visible command/feedback envelope. Error 42 provides firmware feedback-consistency behavior, but the exact electrical transfer and pin/register correspondence remain unresolved.

10. Knock modifies the spark result through `$3FCA` delta consumption and accumulated retard `$00A5`; recovery and Error 43 remain distinct listing-backed paths. U9 pin 42 KNOCK -> `$3FCA` remains a strong inference.

11. The correct simulation architecture keeps periodic scheduler time and engine-event time separate: raw reference events/state at F2, firmware-exact consumers at F0, optional engine dynamics at F3, undocumented U9/U12 transformations at F4.

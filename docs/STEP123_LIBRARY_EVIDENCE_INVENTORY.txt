Cleanup gate

exact hashing and one of these recorded outcomes: committed evidence; proved
byte-identical duplicate of committed/preserved evidence; deliberately excluded
with a reason; or contextual material retained by a sufficient provenance
record. Filename similarity and equal size are not enough.

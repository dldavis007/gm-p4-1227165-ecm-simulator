BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 135 - ESC/KNOCK SIGNAL-CHAIN AUDIT
========================================================

Purpose
-------
Step 135 separates the CAL29/U10 analog ESC monitor from the CAL32/U12/U9
knock-event path and traces the supported firmware behavior through retard,
recovery, spark subtraction, and Error 43.

Findings
--------
1. CAL29 belongs to the analog ESC# conditioning network. Its ESC result reaches
   U10 AN9/selector $90. The supplied normal image never explicitly requests
   $90; factory test stores it at $0184.

2. CAL32 and external KNOCK# meet at U12 pin 15. U12 pin 14 outputs KNOCK to U9
   pin 42. Threshold, polarity, filtering, and CAL32's exact role are not shown.

3. Firmware $D0D1-$D157 uses changes in U9-window counter $3FCA as the knock
   event quantity, applies qualifications and RPM-indexed attack, stores retard
   at $00A5, and subtracts half that value from spark.

4. Segment A $EB3A-$EB59 recovers knock retard. $EB5B-$EBB1 evaluates counter
   activity for Error 43, and $E6DF-$E6E9 latches the fault.

5. The U9 KNOCK-pin-to-$3FCA relationship is strongly inferred from coherent
   endpoints and executable use, but the pin-to-register mapping is not
   documented. The analog AN9 path is not substituted for this missing link.

Source and baseline impact
--------------------------
Documentation/evidence only. No C source, tests, generated outputs, or frozen
behavioral baselines changed.

Conclusion
----------
Step 135 establishes two distinct ESC/knock electrical paths and the exact
processor-visible knock behavior while preserving U12/U9 internal conversion
as an unresolved boundary.

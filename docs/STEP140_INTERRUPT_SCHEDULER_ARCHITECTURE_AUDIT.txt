Step 140 — Interrupt and Scheduler Architecture Audit

Objective
---------
Consolidate the established ordinary IRQ cadence, odd/even minor-loop structure, sixteen-way major dispatcher, common-service ordering, and event-driven hardware boundaries into theory-of-operation Chapter 5.

Evidence reviewed
-----------------
- authoritative scheduler/listing evidence already cross-referenced in `docs/reference/HARDWARE_FIRMWARE_CROSS_REFERENCE.md`
- `src/scheduler_serial.inc.h`
- `tests/scheduler_regression.inc.h`
- U9/U11/U12 architecture from Steps 136-139
- vector/exception evidence from Step 119

Findings
--------
1. Ordinary IRQ service is established at 6.25 ms.

2. Exactly one alternating minor side is selected on each ordinary IRQ. The odd Air/Fuel and even Spark/O2/reference sides therefore each execute at 12.5-ms cadence.

3. Common services are not modeled as independent timers. Established common work such as scheduler-facing IAC service and MAF work occurs at the ordinary 6.25-ms level before/around the minor split according to source ordering.

4. The scheduler state's low nibble selects one of sixteen major segments. Regression evidence verifies each segment is selected once in sixteen IRQs. Therefore each major segment has a mechanically derived nominal 100-ms cadence.

5. Distributor/reference timing is event-driven state supplied through the U12/U9 path and must remain separate from the periodic scheduler clock. Firmware consumption of `$3FFA`, `$3FC0`, `$3FC8` and RPM/dwell state does not prove U9's internal counter implementation.

6. CAL42/U11 OSC and CAL56/U12 CYL remain separate from ordinary IRQ generation and distributor-reference timing unless direct evidence later joins them.

7. Normal 160-baud output uses ordinary-IRQ scheduling plus hardware compare events; those compare events are not additional scheduler loops. 8192-baud SCI behavior remains separately event driven.

8. The deterministic PC architecture should be hierarchical: 6.25-ms IRQ -> common work -> odd/even minor -> one major segment. External reference/VSS/serial/compare events update processor-visible state independently and are consumed by translated firmware in source order.

Regression support
------------------
The existing scheduler regression explicitly verifies:
- 16 IRQ calls -> 16 ordinary IRQ services;
- IAC scheduler-facing service on every ordinary IRQ;
- eight odd and eight even selections in 16 IRQs;
- exactly one call to every major segment in 16 IRQs;
- odd/even phase returns after 16 IRQs;
- common MAF work remains 6.25-ms while the odd fuel chain remains 12.5-ms;
- slow-O2 and connected reference/RPM/dwell work execute on the even cadence under their established conditions.

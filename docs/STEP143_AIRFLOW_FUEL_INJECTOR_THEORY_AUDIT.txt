Findings
--------
1. The supplied image's ordinary fuel path begins with VMAF/U10 AN10, raw `$00ED`, processed airflow `$00EA-$00EB`, and load `$0063` produced from airflow and reference period `$0095-$0096`.

2. MAP/MAP2 and CAL61/U11 MAP configuration remain separate from the executable principal load/fuel producer.

3. Cranking/startup fuel and normal-running fuel are mode-selected firmware paths, not one universal pulse-width equation.

4. Normal fuel preserves source-order AFR/enrichment selection, base pulse-width production, BLM/closed-loop correction, transient/AE addition, and final output shaping/gating.

5. Fast O2 rich/lean state is produced before the closed-loop gate. Closed-loop pulse-width correction remains firmware state rather than a host-side controller.

6. BLM cell selection at `$DA73-$DB35` uses RPM and whole airflow for this calibration (`LC017=0`), with a 4x4/16-cell layout, hysteresis, learning-delay state, bounds/reinitialization behavior, and PE handling. BLM is distinct from fast closed-loop correction.

7. DFCO remains a qualified state machine that suppresses fuel through firmware behavior; it is not reduced to a host-side deceleration flag.

8. Transient/acceleration enrichment is a firmware contribution applied before final injector output shaping and remains distinct from steady-state AFR selection.

9. Batch injector service is gated through processor-visible status represented by `$00A0 bit6`; `$F9D2-$F9E4` writes synchronous pulse width to U9 `$3FD0`. Factory test independently exercises `$3FD0` and EFI-delay `$3FCE`.

10. The hardware envelope U9 `INJS/INJA` -> U12 injector driver -> `INJOUT`/Q1 is direct schematic evidence. U11 `INJ` -> `INJLIMP` and U12 `~LIMP` support a limiting/control relationship, but internal algorithms, phase, polarity, bank meaning, and current control remain F4.

11. Periodic fuel calculation and physical injector-event timing are intentionally separated. U9 event/status gating synchronizes injector service; a new calculated pulse width does not itself imply an immediate physical pulse.

12. No new executable behavior was discovered that requires C or regression-baseline changes.

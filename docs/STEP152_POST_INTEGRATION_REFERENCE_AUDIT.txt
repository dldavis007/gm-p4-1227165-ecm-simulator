Step 152 — Post-Integration Reference-Set Audit

Objective
---------
Audit the completed sixteen-part theory-of-operation structure after Step 151, verify that the integrated reference set is navigable and evidence-bounded, and identify any organizational gaps without inventing new firmware or hardware conclusions.

Findings
--------
1. Steps 139-151 added thirteen dedicated integrated theory documents covering planned chapters 3 and 5-16. Chapter 4 is represented by the pre-existing MEMCAL architecture/functional-network reference set. Chapters 1 and 2 are represented by existing foundational documents rather than by separately named chapter files.

2. The Step-151 statement that the sixteen-part set is closed is therefore organizationally valid, but the index is asymmetric: chapters 1 and 2 have only prose scope bullets while later chapters name their controlling reference documents. This can make those two chapters appear unfinished to a reader even though their evidence is present elsewhere.

3. Chapter 1 is controlled by docs/reference/README.md, docs/reference/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md, docs/reference/HARDWARE_FIRMWARE_CROSS_REFERENCE.md and the theory index. These establish target scope, evidence order, status/confidence vocabulary, source provenance rules and the F0-F4 fidelity vocabulary.

4. Chapter 2 spans both hardware and firmware boundaries. Hardware power/reset/keep-alive evidence belongs in the hardware/evidence references and cross-reference; executable reset, retained-memory validation/recovery, key-off, SWI and restart behavior is integrated in STARTUP_SHUTDOWN_EXCEPTIONAL_MODES.md. The physical reset controller, rail timing and keep-alive consequences remain F4 where not directly established.

5. Chapter 4 is intentionally a reference set rather than one newly generated Step-139+ chapter. MEMCAL_ARCHITECTURE.md supplies the 66-contact carrier/J4 and EPROM architecture; MEMCAL_FUNCTIONAL_NETWORKS.md and MEMCAL_MOTHERBOARD_FIRMWARE_PATHS.md supply functional electrical and firmware-facing traces. Photograph/direct physical evidence remains authoritative for the as-built reconstruction where legible.

6. The evidence hierarchy remains coherent across the integrated set: emitted listing behavior is authoritative for firmware; direct physical/schematic evidence controls hardware connectivity; simulation assumptions remain F3; undocumented custom/external behavior remains F4. No audit finding justifies collapsing those layers.

7. The post-integration set preserves the major unresolved boundaries rather than hiding them: U9/U11/U12 internals, optional $5800 ROM, external $6000 code, exact ALDL electrical behavior, physical reset/keep-alive effects, unresolved MEMCAL destinations/encodings, exact transmission hydraulics and formal exhaustive path coverage.

8. Step 151 correctly distinguishes deterministic behavioral regression from physical truth. Frozen signatures are broad change detectors, not evidence that every simulated or hashed state is factory-accurate.

9. The Step 139-151 comparison is documentation/reference work: it added the integrated theory and audit documents and updated navigation/cross-reference material. This post-integration audit introduces no behavioral baseline change.

10. No new Chapter 17 is warranted. Future numbered work should be evidence-, correction-, implementation- or verification-driven. A useful future navigation cleanup would make chapters 1, 2 and 4 explicitly identify their controlling reference sets in the index, but that cosmetic cleanup is not required for technical closure.

Executable comparison corrections
---------------------------------
The factory-test gate follows the emitted unsigned comparisons, not conflicting
comments. It is reached only when battery A/D is below 100, pump A/D is at
least 160, and diagnostic A/D is in the inclusive/exclusive range 40..99.
Equality at battery 100, pump 159, diagnostic 39, or diagnostic 100 bypasses
factory selection as dictated by BCC/BCS.

Integration boundary
--------------------
The source-ordered evaluator is called by the normal PC reset. Its normal-path
application deliberately writes only values already established by the frozen
PC reset outcome. Decisions for hardware and incomplete firmware areas are
observable in BuaStartupResult112 rather than being simulated speculatively.
This preserves existing behavioral signatures while making the startup route
available for later exact subsystem integration.

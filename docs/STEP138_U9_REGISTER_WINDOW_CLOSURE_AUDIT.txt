Purpose

without assigning semantics to addresses that the repository has not already

Findings
--------
1. The currently established U9 processor-visible addresses remain:
   $3FC0, $3FC8, $3FCA, $3FCE, $3FD0, $3FDC, $3FE4, $3FE6, $3FE8,
   $3FEC, $3FF6, $3FFA and $3FFC.

2. Their supported roles remain reference/counter state, knock-event state,
   injector timing/command, spark/dwell scheduling, and aggregate/control
   status as documented in Step 137.

3. No additional address is assigned a role solely because it lies inside the
   decoded $3FC0-$3FFF hardware window. Every other location remains
   unclassified rather than being called unused, reserved, mirrored, read-only,
   write-only, or variant-specific.

4. The direct hardware pins IGN, INJS, INJA, INJREF, IGNREF, ESTLOOP and KNOCK
   remain separate evidence from the direct firmware register semantics.
   Pin-to-register correspondence remains an undocumented custom-device boundary
   except for explicit strong inferences already recorded.

5. This closure does not justify any C source or baseline change. Simulator
   behavior should remain limited to listing-backed processor-visible semantics
   and raw HAL stimuli/observations.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Step 138 is a closure of the current evidence set, not a claim that the entire
physical U9 register specification has been recovered. An unclassified address
may be hardware-implemented and simply unused by this calibration. Future
verified listings, independent U9 documentation, or controlled pin/register
measurements can reopen the map without invalidating this checkpoint.

Source and baseline impact
--------------------------
Documentation only. No C source, tests, generated output, or frozen behavioral
baseline changed.

Conclusion
----------
The U9 window now has a bounded, evidence-supported register map suitable for
theory-of-operation work. Known processor-visible semantics are documented;
all remaining locations and internal pin/register transformations remain
explicitly unresolved rather than guessed.

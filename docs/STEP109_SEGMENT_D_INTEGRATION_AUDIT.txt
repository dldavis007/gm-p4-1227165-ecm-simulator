Important executable details
----------------------------
Code 12 is emitted unconditionally when a new flash sequence begins. It is not
conditioned on a stored ERR12 bit. After its third repetition, the sequencer
advances through the stored-error masks.

The 6801 ROLA operations retain carry produced by preceding CMP instructions.
For non-group scan indices below 34, that carry is shifted into bit 0 of L00BC.
It cannot propagate to carry-out before the next error-byte reload, so it does
not create a false code, but Step 109 preserves it because L00BC is observable
source RAM.

Scheduler and baseline boundary
-------------------------------
Normal `ecm_reset()` enables the completed Segment-D implementation. Segment D
therefore runs once per 16 ordinary 6.25-ms IRQs, or every 100 ms.

The Step-104 and Step-105 functions explicitly enable a PC-only legacy replay
switch around their historical traces. That switch suppresses newly added
Segment-D behavior only for comparison with those already-frozen signatures.
It is not source RAM, calibration behavior, or a target-HAL assumption.

Purpose
-------
This documentation-only audit revisits the Step-110 whole-image coverage map
after Steps 111 through 121. It asks whether any reachable, internally defined
software behavior in the supplied image is still known to be absent from the
behavioral C port. It does not modify behavioral code.

Classification
--------------
  TRANSLATED   Source behavior is represented in the live behavioral core.
  DATA         Calibration, lookup table, identifier, checksum, or literal data.
  HAL          Software-visible reads/writes are represented; physical effect is external.
  BOUNDARY     Required bytes, circuitry, or processor behavior are not in this PROM.
  UNPORTED     Reachable internally defined executable behavior has no current model.

Remaining evidence boundaries
-----------------------------
1. The two vector destinations at $6000 are outside the supplied PROM.
2. Optional heads-up-display code in the $5800 window is absent.
3. The processor's exception-entry, COP, clock-fail, reset, and stack mechanics
   cannot be proved from application bytes alone.
4. Internal behavior of custom MPU/peripheral devices is represented only to
   the extent established by executable register use and independent hardware
   evidence.
5. SCI/ALDL and FMD electrical timing, transceivers, wiring, and analog effects
   belong to target-specific HALs.
6. Output polarity, driver response, ignition/injector electrical timing, A/D
   transfer functions, and physical plants are not software algorithms.
7. Factory wait-loop duration and hardware stimulus consequences remain HAL
   boundaries even though their software control and raw values are modeled.

Closure conclusion
------------------
No known reachable, internally defined executable area remains classified as
UNPORTED after Step 121. This is strongly supported rather than absolute:
future byte-level control-flow tooling or new primary evidence could identify
a missed side effect. The conclusion means another behavioral translation step
should require a specific listing-backed gap; it does not authorize invented
behavior for any boundary above.

This audit does not claim byte-for-byte emulation, cycle accuracy, complete
processor emulation, exact electrical behavior, exact calibration identity,
or equivalence to the absent optional ROM. Behavioral translation and semantic
identification remain separate claims.

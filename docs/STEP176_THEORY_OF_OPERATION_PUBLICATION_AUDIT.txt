BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 176 - THEORY-OF-OPERATION PUBLICATION AUDIT
================================================================

Purpose
-------
Step 176 performs the publication review of the formal theory of operation.
It changes no C source, tests, calibration, executable behavior or frozen
signature.

Editorial review
----------------
- Removed development-step language from the controlled publication while
  retaining historical milestones in their separate audit files.
- Standardized firmware address notation and subsystem terminology.
- Checked internal references and preserved the distinction among F0
  listing-backed behavior, F1 proven hardware connections, F2 raw HAL state,
  F3 simulation assumptions and F4 unresolved/external behavior.
- Expanded the executable-anchor appendix into a consolidated RAM,
  processor-visible register and executable-region index.
- Added a publication glossary while preserving the detailed reference
  chapters as the address-level authority.

PDF publication
---------------
THEORY_OF_OPERATION.pdf is the stable 13-page Letter-size publication of the
Markdown master. It includes a cover, generated contents, linked references,
consistent tables, repeated table headings, headers, footers and page numbers.
The PDF is generated reproducibly by tools/generate_theory_of_operation_pdf.py.

Visual verification
-------------------
All 13 pages were rendered to PNG with Poppler and visually inspected. Cover,
contents, section transitions, tables, page breaks, hyperlinks, headers,
footers, and page numbering were legible with no clipping, overlap, missing
glyphs, black squares or unintended blank pages. PDF text extraction contained
no replacement characters, placeholders or development-step language.

Evidence preservation
---------------------
The authoritative firmware source remains evidence/firmware/bua-hac.lst.
bua-hac.txt remains excluded. The publication does not invent U9/U11/U12
internals, selector $B0, optional $5800 code, external $6000 behavior,
electrical polarity, actuator dynamics or plant truth.

Verification
------------
All new relative links were checked against repository paths. The publication
generator produced identical output on consecutive runs. The unchanged source
was rebuilt with strict C89 flags and the complete make test gate was run.
Compiler diagnostics were empty, runtime exit status was zero, all gated
results and signatures passed, and no genuine FAIL result was present.

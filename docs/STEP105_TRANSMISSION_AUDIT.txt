Model boundary
--------------
The transmission and vehicle trajectory are PC plant behavior, not translated
ECM code.  Vehicle speed, throttle A/D, and MAF A/D remain deterministic test
stimuli.  While the selector is in Drive, RPM is not independently prescribed.
It is derived from:

  vehicle speed
  selected transmission ratio
  combined axle/tire conversion factor
  unlocked torque-converter slip
  physical TCC state

The result drives the phase-continuous distributor-reference source.  The ECM
therefore obtains RPM through its normal reference-period calculation.

Explicit simulator parameters
-----------------------------
Four forward internal ratios:
  1st 3.06, 2nd 1.62, 3rd 1.00, 4th 0.70

Upshift thresholds:
  1-2 at 16 MPH, 2-3 at 30 MPH, 3-4 at 45 MPH

Downshift thresholds:
  4-3 below 38 MPH, 3-2 below 25 MPH, 2-1 below 12 MPH

The combined axle/tire factor is selected so locked fourth gear produces
N/V=32.  This is centered in the executable LC288..LC289 recognition window
of 30..35.  Converter-slip values and the shift speeds are test parameters.
They are not presented as verified production calibration for a particular
1986 Corvette axle, tire, governor, or throttle-valve adjustment.

Source-shaped N/V path
----------------------
The supplied assembly calculates L008F by loading VSS Q8.8 from L0065,
constructing approximately RPM from L0057*25, and calling LF215 FDIV.  The high
byte returned in A is effectively the integer RPM/MPH ratio.  Step 105
reproduces that scaling from ECM-measured RPM and VSS; it does not pass an
ordinary unscaled gear number into the classifier.

TCC boundary
------------
The already-translated N/V fourth-gear confirmation and TCC decision helpers
execute at the 100-ms major-loop cadence.  Physical clutch energization remains
separate: the ECM command and external brake-switch power must both be present.
The profile deliberately opens brake power during cruise and then restores it.

Remaining limitation
--------------------
This is a drivetrain-kinematics integration model, not a predictive vehicle
dynamics model.  It does not calculate acceleration from engine torque,
vehicle mass, aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance, or hydraulic line
pressure.  Those omissions are deliberate and are not silently filled with
guessed Corvette values.

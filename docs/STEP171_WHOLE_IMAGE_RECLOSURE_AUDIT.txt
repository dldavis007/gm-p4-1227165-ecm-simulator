BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 171 - WHOLE-IMAGE RECLOSURE AUDIT
=======================================================

Purpose
-------
Step 171 reconciles the whole-image closure record after Steps 168-170 resolve
the exact 160-baud LF880 exception found by Step 167. It is a documentation-only
audit and changes no C behavior, tables, tests or frozen signatures.

Authority and method
--------------------
The authoritative firmware evidence remains evidence/firmware/bua-hac.lst.
Emitted instructions, bytes, resolved targets, address use and tables outrank
comments. bua-hac.txt is not used. BUA remains a source label rather than an
independently proved calibration identity.

This audit starts from the Step-122 region map, applies the Step-167 correction,
and verifies the dispositions recorded by Steps 168, 169 and 170. It does not
infer behavior for absent ROM, undocumented devices or external circuitry.

Resolved Step-167 exception
---------------------------
Step 167 found two reachable, internally defined LF880 branches that the then
normal-only C routine returned around:

1. Diagnostic table manager $F8CB-$F8ED.
   Step 168 translates indices 1-24 through the emitted $C70D-$C73C address
   table and preserves the index-25 common reset/$FF state.

2. Factory table manager $F88E-$F8B4.
   Step 169 translates all three 16-byte streams rooted at $FC72, $FC82 and
   $FC92, including even-index word fetch, odd-index $017A carry, and the
   index-16 common reset/$FF state.

Step 170 then exposes the complete normal/diagnostic/factory byte manager at a
cell-level raw HAL while leaving byte selection and ownership in LF880. Its
tests observe raw line and manager state without assigning connector voltage
polarity or external transceiver behavior.

Reconciled classification
-------------------------
The Step-122 region map and translated/HAL/data/boundary classifications remain
valid. The sole later-discovered UNPORTED exception, $F88E-$F8B4 and
$F8CB-$F8ED, is now TRANSLATED. No other exact listing-backed discrepancy has
been established by the subsequent hardware, theory or HAL audits.

The following remain explicit boundaries rather than unported application
behavior:

- vector destinations at $6000 and optional HUD code at $5800, whose bytes are
  absent from the supplied PROM image;
- processor exception-entry, COP, clock-fail, reset and stack mechanics;
- undocumented U9/U11/U12 and other custom-device internal transfer behavior;
- target-specific A/D, timer, retained-power and output-driver implementation;
- SCI/ALDL/FMD transceivers, voltage polarity, collision/loading behavior and
  physical timing; and
- physical plant, actuator, ignition, injection and hydraulic consequences.

Closure conclusion
------------------
No known reachable, internally defined executable area in the supplied image
remains classified as UNPORTED after Step 170. This is an evidence-supported,
qualified behavioral closure, not a claim of byte-for-byte processor emulation,
cycle accuracy, complete electrical emulation, exact calibration identity or
equivalence to absent external ROM.

Any future behavioral translation must begin with a newly identified exact
listing range and executable discrepancy. Hardware integration may continue at
the established HAL boundaries without changing this software conclusion.

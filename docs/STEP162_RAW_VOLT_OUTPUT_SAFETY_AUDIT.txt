BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 162 - RAW VOLT OUTPUT SAFETY AUDIT
======================================================

Purpose
-------
Step 162 proves a complete raw input-to-output safety path. Raw U10 AN1/VOLT is
acquired by ordinary Segment E, then consumed by the listing's two-pass
overvoltage qualification in Segment 1. The regression observes only established
raw MPU and parallel-I/O outputs through the Step-128 HAL boundary.

Source sequence
---------------
Segment E acquires U10 selector $10 and stores the raw result in $007E. Segment
1 later compares $007E with literal 171 at $EDA3-$EDA7.

For a value below 171, $EDBA clears $0035 bit 6 and output staging proceeds
normally. At 171 or above, the first Segment-1 pass sets $003E bit 4 and, because
$0035 bit 6 was clear, sets that qualification bit without forcing outputs off.

On the next consecutive high-voltage Segment-1 pass, $0035 bit 6 is already set.
The source sets $004F bit 6 and selects the common $D000 output path. That path
writes $D000 to $3FD2, $3FD6, $3FD8, $3FCC, and $3FD4, then clears parallel-I/O
$4004 bit 1.

When raw VOLT returns below 171, the next Segment-1 pass clears $0035 bit 6 and
normal output staging resumes. The executable does not clear $003E bit 4 or
$004F bit 6 in this block, so Step 162 verifies that those flags remain latched
rather than inventing recovery behavior.

Regression stimulus and result
------------------------------
The regression starts through the real power-on composition, establishes an
engine-running state with known active AIR, acceleration-enrichment, TCC, purge,
EGR, and fan requests, and drives these four output stages through ordinary
Segment-E and Segment-1 scheduler dispatch:

1. raw VOLT 170, below threshold;
2. first raw VOLT 171 Segment-1 pass;
3. second consecutive raw VOLT 171 Segment-1 pass;
4. raw VOLT recovery to 170.

Ten checks prove:

- Segment E acquires raw values 170 and 171 at the expected boundary;
- below-threshold active raw outputs are present;
- the first high pass sets qualification and burnoff-disable state but does not
  prematurely force outputs off;
- the second high pass latches the error and forced-off state;
- all five PWM counters become $D000 and the fan bit clears;
- recovery clears qualification and restores the known active output pattern;
- the source-latched burnoff-disable and error bits remain set.

The FNV-1a signature covers all five raw counters plus $0035, $003E, $004F, and
$4004 at each of the four stages:

  Step-162 raw-VOLT output-safety signature: DB86F332
  step-162 raw-VOLT output-safety regression result: PASS (10/10)

The test saves and restores memory, statistics, lifecycle/vector/power/startup
traces, output-stage state, raw VOLT, and the software-powerdown latch.

Evidence boundary
-----------------
The literal 171 is a raw firmware comparison. Although the source comment calls
it 17.1 VDC, Step 162 does not add a physical voltage conversion, divider model,
tolerance, transient response, output polarity, relay state, or load behavior.
All observed output values remain processor-visible F0/F2 quantities.

Conclusion
----------
Step 162 connects the named raw VOLT HAL input to an independently frozen raw
output safety consequence through the ordinary scheduler. It proves the exact
two-pass threshold behavior and recovery sequence without promoting comments or
unverified electrical behavior above the executable listing.

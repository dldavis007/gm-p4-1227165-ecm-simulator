BUA / GM 1227165 STEP 170 - COMPLETE 160-BAUD RAW HAL AUDIT
=============================================================

Purpose
-------
Steps 168 and 169 closed the diagnostic and factory LF880 gaps identified by
Step 167. Step 170 now exposes the complete translated 160-baud manager through
a narrow cell-level raw HAL.

Boundary
--------
The HAL initializes L0189-L018B and the serial flags, advances one complete
LCAA4/LCA2C/LCA3B cell at a caller-supplied timer origin, and exposes the raw
line, table index, bit count and transmit byte. Selection and byte ownership
remain in LF880: ordinary display, 25-byte diagnostic, and each of the three
16-byte factory streams.

Regression
----------
Nine Strict C89 checks cover reset state, a full normal byte reconstructed only
from cell trace bits, terminal line/flag state, diagnostic selection, factory
even/odd L017A carry, and load-cell trace behavior. Existing frozen signatures
and all prior regression gates remain unchanged.

This seam does not assign connector voltage polarity, reproduce the external
transceiver, model cable loading or collision behavior, or prescribe a target
timer driver. Those remain hardware responsibilities.
